# Evidence and Claims

| Evidence | Supports | Does not support |
|---|---|---|
| Unit or contract test | Engineering behavior under tested conditions | Scientific effectiveness |
| Smoke run | Executable pipeline and artifact validity | Comparative performance |
| Exploration | Diagnostic observation and a candidate hypothesis | Confirmatory comparison |
| Pilot | Preregistered advancement or no-go decision | Stable or population-level superiority |
| Formal repeated comparison | Bounded comparison under the registered design | Unmeasured domains or real-world deployment |
| Stress test | Robustness in named stress conditions | Universal robustness |
| Mechanism diagnostic | Evidence for or against a candidate explanation | Causality without an identified design |

Every consequential statement should identify its class: code fact, engineering validation, measured research evidence, external-source claim, research hypothesis, or interpretive inference. Preserve negative, invalid, and inconclusive results. State limitations and the next evidence needed beside the current claim.
